# M1 — Holding-period & tax drag — FINDINGS

id: momentum_enh_M1_tax_holding_period
date: 2026-07-30
mechanism: M1 (momentum enhancement batch)
hypothesis: >
  Turnover-reduction levers — slower rebalance cadence, and decile-boundary
  hysteresis banding — preserve >=90% of the frozen baseline's GROSS Sharpe
  while MATERIALLY REDUCING after-tax drag under India's 20%/12.5% split.
category: momentum / tax-efficiency / turnover
mechanism_tags: turnover_reduction, tax_drag, holding_period, cadence, banding, weighting

data_and_universe: >
  Nifty 500 PIT universe, v3 panel (feature_panel_with_pit_v2, 3,328,132 rows,
  2010-01-04 → 2026-07-10). 12-1 momentum, decile long-short. PIT filter
  in_nifty500_flag == 1. 8 corporate-action tickers screened from return paths;
  vendor-failure date excluded from the signal window.
  NOTE ON BASELINE: M1 measures every variant MONTHLY (mark-to-market each
  month, trade only on rebalance dates) so cadence variants are comparable. This
  reaches the panel's final date and yields a 186-month baseline at gross Sharpe
  0.910 — vs Phase B's 179-month / 0.847 frozen reference, which used a
  forward-return convention that stopped ~7 months earlier. The per-month spread
  (~1.74-1.84%/mo) reproduces the frozen strategy; only the sample boundary
  differs. M1 preservation %s compare each variant to M1's OWN 186-month
  baseline (internally consistent). Do NOT cross-compare M1 absolute Sharpes to
  the Phase B findings doc without accounting for the 7-month difference.

tax_model: >
  Honest baseline, not optimised: flat 20% if lot held <=365 days, else 12.5%.
  No Rs 1.25 lakh exemption, no loss-harvesting ordering. FIFO within name.
  Long leg only taxed; short leg reported gross. Losses taxed symmetrically.
  Capital base Rs 1 crore (documented; scales tax vs gross). Lot ledger
  reconciled to the paisa (Rs 36,383,269.22 realized gain, independent recompute
  identical).

result: >
  DECISION: REJECT. No cadence variant both preserves >=90% gross Sharpe AND
  materially reduces after-tax drag. A pincer:
    - cadences fast enough to preserve Sharpe are too fast to reach the 365-day
      LTCG threshold, so tax saving ~= 0;
    - the cadence slow enough to shift gains long-term (semiannual) fails
      preservation.

  Cadence (measured monthly; % of 186-month baseline gross Sharpe 0.910):
    bimonthly   grossSR 0.895  (98.3%)  turn 4.71 (base 6.81)  LTCG 6.2%   dAfterTax -0.02%/mo
    quarterly   grossSR 0.909  (99.9%)  turn 3.85              LTCG 8.6%   dAfterTax +0.02%/mo
    semiannual  grossSR 0.635  (69.8%)  turn 2.75              LTCG 16.3%  dAfterTax -0.54%/mo
  Quarterly preserves essentially all the gross Sharpe but saves ~nothing after
  tax (still 91% short-term); semiannual finally reaches LTCG but the six-month-
  old signal goes stale and the edge collapses.

  Banding (monthly cadence, lazy exit; % of baseline):
    (10,8)  grossSR 0.767 (84.3%)  turn 3.45  LTCG 14.0%
    (10,7)  grossSR 0.708 (77.8%)  turn 3.03  LTCG 19.8%
    (10,9)  grossSR 0.816 (89.7%)  turn 4.27  LTCG  9.5%
  None cleanly clears 90%. All after-tax deltas negative. Hold-out degrades hard
  ((10,8) train 0.83 -> holdout 0.62; (10,7) 0.78 -> 0.53), i.e. banding both
  fails preservation and overfits. REJECT.

significance_provisional: >
  Deflated Sharpe Ratio, provisional at 6 trials (recompute at batch end with
  final N). Gross DSR: cadence bimonthly 0.968, quarterly 0.976, semiannual
  0.865; band (10,8) 0.931, (10,7) 0.900, (10,9) 0.948. These confirm the
  underlying spreads are individually significant — NOT that the hypothesis
  holds. The rejection is on the joint preserve-AND-cut-tax claim, which no
  variant satisfies regardless of DSR.

holdout: >
  2022-2026 single look. Baseline train 0.927 -> holdout 0.990. Quarterly
  0.959 -> 0.846 (preservation real out-of-sample, but tax saving was never
  there). Banding degrades sharply (see above). Semiannual weak in both.

validation_summary: >
  coverage: 186-month monthly-mark baseline, all months full ~48-name books,
    0 carried-book anomalies, first/last spread months 2011-02 / 2026-07.
  ledger: sum(per-lot gain) == independent price recompute to Rs 0.01. PASS.
  buckets: 0 holding-day->bucket misclassifications; 0 wrong rate. PASS.
  skip window: exactly 21 rows at every cadence. PASS.
  PIT filter: flagged universe varies across dates (live). PASS.
  baseline anchor: per-month spread 1.84%/mo reproduces frozen strategy
    (convention-independent check; passes even though month count differs).

decision: REJECT (hypothesis fails on its joint claim)

insight: >
  The binding constraint is the 365-day LTCG threshold, NOT turnover. Monthly
  momentum's natural holding period is far short of a year, so gains cannot be
  shifted into the low-tax bucket without slowing to semiannual — at which point
  signal staleness destroys the edge. Tax-efficient momentum in India cannot be
  achieved by cadence or lazy-exit banding alone; it requires a mechanism that
  extends holding PAST one year without going stale, which turnover reduction
  per se does not provide. Separately and unexpectedly: quarterly rebalancing
  preserves ~100% of gross Sharpe — 12-1 momentum is persistent enough that
  quarter-old rankings are barely decayed. That is a genuine (if tax-irrelevant)
  finding about signal persistence.

incidental_observations: >
  (1) signal_prop weighting (M1b) is the one positive: gross Sharpe 0.898 vs
  equal 0.767, train 0.941 -> holdout 0.868 (survives OOS), effN 107 vs 142 (more
  concentrated but not pathological), best after-tax Sharpe in the run (0.714).
  BUT it raises turnover (5.43 vs 3.45) and LOWERS LTCG share (1.9% vs 14%), so it
  works AGAINST the tax objective. It is a WEIGHTING finding that wandered into a
  tax test — held for a dedicated weighting mechanism, NOT promoted here (it was
  never tested against a weighting hypothesis). (2) linear_rank and decile_level
  weighting are close to equal-weight and add nothing. (3) Quarterly-cadence
  Sharpe preservation (~100%) is a reusable fact about 12-1 signal persistence,
  relevant to any future turnover/cost mechanism. (4) After-tax magnitudes depend
  on the Rs 1 crore capital base and the simplified tax model (no exemption, no
  harvesting); a full tax-optimisation could change after-tax levels but CANNOT
  rescue the core finding, since neither the exemption nor harvesting changes the
  holding-period threshold.

flag_for_idea_generator: >
  Tax-efficient momentum needs a mechanism that lengthens holding PAST 365 days
  without going stale — cadence cannot (fast=stale-safe-but-short-hold,
  slow=long-hold-but-stale). Candidate directions: a two-sleeve design (a fast
  core for signal freshness + a slow sleeve that only sells lots crossing the
  1-year mark), or tax-lot-aware exit timing (defer a sale a few weeks when a lot
  is near 365 days AND still ranks). Both are green-field, route to Idea-Generator
  not implementer. SEPARATELY: signal_prop weighting deserves its own mechanism
  tested against a weighting hypothesis (gross Sharpe + concentration + hold-out),
  not tax.
